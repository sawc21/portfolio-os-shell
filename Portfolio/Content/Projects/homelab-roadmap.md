# Homelab Roadmap

The public portfolio should stay on managed hosting, but self-hosting still belongs in the story. The right pattern is to treat self-hosted work as a showcase and learning track instead of the dependency that keeps the portfolio online.

## Planned experiments

- Cloudflare Tunnel for private service access.
- A VPS-hosted ASP.NET API for small dynamic features.
- A status page that reports uptime and deployment notes.
- Raspberry Pi services for local automation and learning.

## Product boundary

Self-hosting is a credibility layer, not the main risk surface. Visitors should see infrastructure curiosity and operational thinking without depending on a home server to load the portfolio.

## Boundary

The portfolio remains the reliable public front door. The homelab becomes documented evidence of infrastructure skill, not a single point of failure.
